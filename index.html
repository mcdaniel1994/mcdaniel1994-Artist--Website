01 — About

About Me

I photograph the world the way memory works — selective, luminous, and a little blurred at the edges.

Born in Seville and based in New York City, I've spent two decades chasing light across five continents. My practice centers on long-exposure portraiture and architectural abstraction, finding the human in the monumental and the monumental in the mundane.

My work has been exhibited at the MoMA PS1, the Tate Modern, and the Museo Nacional Centro de Arte Reina Sofía. In 2023, Aperture Foundation published my first monograph, Luz y Sombra.

When I'm not behind the lens, I teach advanced darkroom techniques at the International Center of Photography in Manhattan.

02 — Career

Career Highlights

Over 20 years of work across fine art, editorial, and documentary photography.

  1. 2024

    Between Breaths — MoMA PS1

    Solo exhibition of 40 large-format prints exploring urban solitude. Ran for 14 weeks; attended by over 60,000 visitors.

  2. 2023

    Aperture Monograph: Luz y Sombra

    Published by Aperture Foundation. Selected as one of Time magazine's "10 Best Photography Books of the Year."

  3. 2022

    World Press Photo — 1st Place, Portrait

    Won first place in the Portrait Stories category for the series Mujeres del Puerto, documenting fishing communities in Cádiz.

  4. 2021

    Tate Modern — Group Exhibition: New Cartographies

    One of six artists selected to represent emerging voices in contemporary documentary photography.

  5. 2018

    National Geographic — Artist in Residence

    Six-month residency resulting in the 12-page feature The Last Shepherds of the Sierra, reaching 4.2 million readers.

  6. 2014

    ICP — Faculty Appointment

    Appointed adjunct faculty at the International Center of Photography, teaching Advanced Darkroom and Fine Art Printing.
